# TinyZKP Guard founding validation protocol

This is a one-time product-qualification program, permanently capped at three
legal organizations. It is not consulting, a pilot product, a custom
integration offer, or a recurring sales motion.

## Entry requirements

An organization may be considered only when all of the following are true:

- it already uses the published `tinyzkp-p3-goldilocks-v1` profile;
- it has a real prover OOM, memory-cost, or interruption/restart problem;
- it runs the free doctor locally and supplies only its reviewed, scrubbed
  compatibility report;
- it confirms in writing that the standard annual price is $4,990;
- it submits at most one workload through the public adapter and
  `JobManifestV1`; and
- it accepts the ordinary product boundary without an SLA, custom AIR work,
  private branch, hosted compute, security questionnaire, or architecture
  review.

Never request or accept witness data, trace chunks, proof secrets, production
credentials, license keys, proprietary source, arbitrary Plonky3 forks, or
unsupported AIR features.

## Assistance limit

TinyZKP assistance is capped at four engineering hours per organization,
including diagnosis and documentation. Reaching that cap without an unaided
verified proof is product evidence that the workflow is not self-service; do
not extend the cap or create a customer-specific branch.

## Evidence recorded

The release gate may record only:

- an opaque organization identifier;
- the scrubbed compatibility report digest;
- the public adapter and schema versions;
- the documented baseline failure class and approximate business cost;
- proof and verifier digests;
- bounded/conventional resource summaries;
- interruption/resume outcome;
- unaided time to verified proof;
- assistance minutes; and
- written standard-price acceptance and ordinary-checkout purchase status.

Evidence must not contain witness values, trace data, source code, private
paths, customer names in public files, email addresses, keys, or credentials.

## Market gate

Commercial development may continue only after:

- three external workloads from at least two organizations;
- one public adapter and job contract for all workloads;
- no customer-specific Guard or engine branch;
- at least two organizations willing to buy the standard annual product; and
- each documented failure cost materially more than the annual price.

At release-candidate stage, at least two organizations must purchase through
the same ordinary hosted checkout used at general availability. If this gate
fails, stop commercial development and retain TinyZKP as an open-source
research project.
