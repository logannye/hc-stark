Commercial guidance

The lowest number is not always the best buying path. TinyZKP is most valuable when the proof receipt reaches the person, agent, auditor, or customer who needs to verify it.

Agent builders

Start Free or Developer while the receipt is internal. Move to Pro when the receipt becomes part of customer-facing agent output.

Read agent guide

Agent platforms

Use the $5,000 production pilot when receipts should become a native platform policy, then move to a sales-assisted platform rollout after verifier and support paths are clear.

Review rollout package

API teams

Use Developer for one production pilot. Move to Pro or Scale once receipts attach to a recurring mutation or contractual workflow.

Audit and finance

Use Pro or Scale for recurring checkpoints. Use Compute when backfills or reserve traces are long and sporadic.

Long traces

Use Compute when paying by trace step beats operating high-RAM workers, queues, and prover support yourself.
