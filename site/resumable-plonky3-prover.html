Resumable Plonky3 prover

Recovery is a release-bound protocol, not a retry flag.

A resumable prover must know which durable phase completed, validate every checkpoint component, and refuse a different release rather than guessing.

Properties that make recovery deterministic

Atomic durable state

A checkpoint becomes visible only after its complete contents and integrity metadata are durable.

Exact release identity

The owning Guard, engine, schemas, proof profile, and artifact digest are recorded and checked before resume.

Fail-closed reconstruction

Missing, truncated, corrupt, stale, symlinked, or cross-release state is rejected rather than repaired silently.

Interruption is not corruption

A signal or host restart after a durable phase may produce interrupted_resumable. Integrity failure produces a different reason and must not be turned into a resume attempt.

Keep the old release

Never auto-delete an artifact that owns an unfinished checkpoint. A newer release must not consume older state silently. Verify the final proof through the released ordinary Plonky3 verifier.
