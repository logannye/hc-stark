Evidence bundle

Identity

Immutable source SHA, release tag, engine and Guard versions, OCI digest, artifact hashes, schemas, and compatibility profile.

Resources

Whole-process peak RSS, elapsed time, CPU time, read and write bytes, scratch peak and estimate, exit class, and cgroup settings.

Correctness

Proof digest, conventional/bounded equality where required, official verifier outcome, crash-resume cases, and integrity failures.

Publication rules

  • Component measurements are labeled as component measurements.
  • Targets are never presented as achieved results.
  • Every result names hardware, profile, workload, source, command, and raw report.
  • Results are not generalized to unsupported AIRs, platforms, fields, hashes, forks, or row counts.
  • Independent reproduction and specialist review are transparent, nonblocking advisory metrics.
