What CI must fail

Memory breach

Peak RSS exceeds the configured cap by more than 10% in an isolated subprocess.

Nondeterministic recovery

Crash/resume or memory/scratch mode changes commitments, challenges, openings, or proof bytes.

Incomplete provenance

A published number lacks hardware, command, source identity, profile, workload, or raw output.
