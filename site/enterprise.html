Rollout shape

Enterprise adoption should still start narrow. The goal is to prove one business-critical workflow, then expand after cost, verification, and support expectations are known.

0-14 daysRun one scoped pilot with success metrics, verifier path, data boundary, and cost estimate.
15-30 daysAttach receipts to one production-like workflow and route security/procurement review through the public packet.
31-60 daysSet reserved capacity, support targets, and commercial terms if the receipt becomes customer- or auditor-visible.
After launchExpand to adjacent workflows only after receipt verification and incident ownership are operationally clear.

Bring this to enterprise review

  • Workflow owner, verifier, and business decision the receipt supports.
  • Expected monthly proof volume, trace-size range, and peak concurrency.
  • Whether the receipt is internal, auditor-visible, customer-visible, or agent-supervisor visible.
  • Security, privacy, legal, retention, invoice, and support requirements.

Do not use enterprise review to bypass product boundaries

  • No blanket private-input ZK claim without a documented template, explicit review status, and supported verifier path.
  • No arbitrary zkVM or zkML availability claim as if it is live self-serve product.
  • No guarantee that off-chain facts are true unless they are encoded into the proven statement.
  • No production commitment before verifier behavior, data boundary, and support path are clear.
