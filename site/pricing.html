What the subscription buys

An activated exact release continues working locally and offline. An active subscription provides the right to activate later qualified releases. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period and never remotely disables a release that was already activated.

Locked price policy

The $499 monthly and $4,990 annual prices are fixed through general availability plus six months; pricing.json publishes the applicable date. Annual is the default. Founding customers use the same undiscounted annual variant. A later price change retains the reviewed merchant variant IDs; Lemon Squeezy preserves each existing subscription's original price, and the IDs are never deleted or repurposed.

Refunds and merchant status

Hosted merchant checkout, invoices, payment updates, renewal, dunning, cancellation, and any legally required refund are handled through the merchant portal. See the refund policy and subscription terms. Final binding terms and seller details must be counsel-approved before checkout can open.
