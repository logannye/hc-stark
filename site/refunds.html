Cancellation

Monthly and annual subscriptions remain active through the end of the paid billing period after cancellation. Renewal stops automatically. Cancellation is available in the merchant's hosted customer portal and does not require contacting TinyZKP.

Refunds

The default is no prorated or discretionary refund for an elapsed billing period, except where required by applicable law or the merchant of record. Any published cooling-off right, defective-product remedy, duplicate-charge correction, or merchant-required refund controls over this summary.

Effect on activated releases

Cancellation or expiration does not remotely disable a Guard release that was successfully activated while the subscription was current. It prevents activation of later releases unless the subscription becomes current again.

Compatibility before purchase

The Community engine, public compatibility matrix, and free doctor are the evaluation path. Buyers are responsible for confirming their platform and workload fit the exact supported release profile before purchase.

Merchant support

For a live checkout, payment corrections, invoices, cancellation, and refund requests are handled through the hosted merchant portal linked from the receipt. TinyZKP does not operate a parallel billing account system.
