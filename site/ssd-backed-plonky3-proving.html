Treat scratch as proof infrastructure.

Bounded execution trades resident memory for customer-owned durable storage. The scratch device, free-space margin, permissions, integrity, and cleanup policy become part of the job contract.

Preflight before ingestion

Capacity

Require the candidate estimate plus at least 10% free-space headroom. Reject the job before expensive work if either budget fails.

Device and path

Use a regular customer-controlled directory under an approved root. Reject traversal, symlinks, and devices.

Durability

Make phase boundaries atomic and checksummed; test Linux ENOSPC and truncation explicitly.

Scratch may be sensitive

TinyZKP makes no zero-knowledge privacy claim. Scratch may contain witness-derived material. Use encrypted storage where appropriate, owner-only permissions, a documented retention period, and media sanitization under your own policy.

Measure both sides of the trade

Record peak RSS, wall time, maximum scratch use, preflight estimate, device identity, and official verification. Published gates require bounded scratch use to remain within 10% of preflight on the fixed host.
