Adapter scope

Scratch-backed Matrix

Implemented sequential creation, bounded block reads, canonical field encoding, checksums, and policy-bound preflight.

Two-adic DFT

The current blockwise radix-2 scratch prototype is differentially tested. The planned tiled row/column transform and double-buffered transpose remain incomplete.

Compatibility matrix

Exact upstream releases, toolchain, dependency lock, artifact serializer, AIR, field, and verifier combinations are pinned in a machine-readable manifest.

Validation workloads

Fibonacci AIR

An upstream-style reference workload that isolates prover compatibility, deterministic proof equality, and storage overhead.

Poseidon2 AIR

A batched Goldilocks hash workload with enough width and rows to measure real scratch, I/O, and RSS behavior.

Upstream first: TinyZKP will open a design RFC after the measured 1M-row prototype and contribute generally useful matrix/storage interfaces where maintainers agree.
