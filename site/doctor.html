The Community engine owns compatibility, automatic mode planning, RAM estimation, and scratch-headroom checks. It rejects unsafe or unsupported jobs before expensive ingestion.

Distribution status: the exact-contract Community doctor has not yet been published as a signed release. Do not treat an unqualified build or copied command as release evidence. The release gate remains blocked.

Expected local flow

tinyzkp-engine doctor --job job.json

The signed evaluation release will emit one versioned DoctorReportV1 result. Keep that report local. Never send its contents, a witness, trace chunk, license key, entitlement, secret, private path, or proprietary AIR source. A founding-validation record may contain only the report digest and standardized compatibility result.

Good fit

Continue evaluating when

  • Your production target is Linux x86-64.
  • Your workload matches the exact published Plonky3 profile.
  • You have a measured OOM, high-memory machine cost, or restart-loss problem.
  • Local NVMe scratch under your control is acceptable.

Stop when

  • You need lookups, buses, multi-table AIRs, recursion, a GPU, or a custom field.
  • You need hosted proving, a service bureau, an SLA, custom AIR work, SSO, or private branches.
  • You cannot keep witness-derived scratch data on customer-managed storage.
  • The doctor reports an unsupported profile or unsafe path.
