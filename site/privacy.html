Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account and contact information: Email address provided during signup, plus any name, message, and optional project-fit details you submit through the contact form.
  • Usage data: API requests (tenant ID, trace length, duration, timestamp). We do not store program contents or proof data after delivery.
  • Product analytics: Low-cardinality website events such as page views, docs copy-button clicks, signup/checkout starts, playground proof completion, and verifier success/failure status. These events do not include proof bytes, API keys, email addresses, or form contents.
  • Billing data: Processed by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers.
  • Client-side verification: The @tinyzkp/verify WASM package runs entirely in your browser or runtime when used directly. The hosted verifier page tries local WASM first but may send pasted proof bytes to TinyZKP's hosted verifier fallback when the local verifier package is behind the current production proof format. The website may log a coarse success/failure analytics event; analytics events do not include proof bytes.

2. How We Use It

  • To provide and bill the Service.
  • To monitor service health and prevent abuse.
  • To understand product activation and improve documentation, pricing, and onboarding.
  • To communicate about your account.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe for payment processing.
  • Law enforcement when legally required.

4. Data Retention

Usage logs are retained for billing and audit purposes. Account data is retained while your account is active and for 90 days after deletion.

5. Security

We use TLS encryption for all API traffic, authenticated access via API keys, and follow security best practices for data storage.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data via our contact form.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via email.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us.
