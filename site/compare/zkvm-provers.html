Comparison

TinyZKP receipts vs general-purpose zkVM provers.

A zkVM is the right abstraction when you need to prove arbitrary program execution. TinyZKP is the right abstraction when the buyer needs a hosted, narrow receipt for a supported state transition, with no prover infrastructure or circuit engineering project.

Choose TinyZKP when

  • The workflow is a checkpoint, accumulator, balance, reserve, inventory, or agent action receipt.
  • You need API/MCP integration more than arbitrary program expressiveness.
  • The verifier should receive a compact receipt URL or object.
  • High-RAM prover operations are not your core business.

Choose a zkVM when

  • You need to prove arbitrary code execution.
  • Your predicate cannot be expressed as a TinyZKP live template or reviewed workload.
  • You need ecosystem-specific on-chain verification or protocol-level proof composition.
  • Your team is ready to own the full proving and verification stack.

Positioning boundary

TinyZKP should not market current self-serve receipts as finished arbitrary zkVM execution. The honest wedge is narrower and stronger: proof receipts for state-changing automated work, plus Compute for long supported traces where memory pressure makes managed proving valuable.
