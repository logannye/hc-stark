Build vs buy

TinyZKP vs operating your own STARK prover stack.

If your buyer question is "why not self-host?", the answer is not only hosting cost. It is prover memory, queueing, monitoring, verifier distribution, proof-format compatibility, support, and the time it takes to turn proof research into a reliable product surface.

Where TinyZKP is the better fit

Long state-transition traces

TinyZKP's supported receipt path is designed around O(√N) prover memory. That matters when conventional O(N) proving pushes you toward high-RAM instances, trace segmentation, and recursive aggregation.

Portable verification

You get hosted verification, a public verifier page, a first-party WASM verifier path where compatible, SDKs, and MCP tooling without building a verifier distribution plan from scratch.

Small or bursty proof volume

Self-hosting is hardest to justify when usage is uneven. TinyZKP lets you start on the free tier, then use monthly receipt tiers or Compute pricing when traces become large.

Fast commercial validation

You can test whether customers, auditors, or agents value transferable receipts before hiring proof-system specialists or operating prover infrastructure.
