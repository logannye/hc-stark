Self-service proving

Validate locally, prepay, and submit with the signed CLI.

TinyZKP accepts declarative Goldilocks AIR and checksummed trace data. It never executes customer-provided code.

Sign in with GitHubOpenAPI 3.1

Quickstart

hc-cli beta quickstart --fixture fibonacci --output-dir tinyzkp-example
export TINYZKP_API_KEY=tzb_...
hc-cli beta submit --air tinyzkp-example/air.json --qualification-trace tinyzkp-example/qualification.trace --qualification-public-inputs tinyzkp-example/qualification-public-inputs.json --job-trace tinyzkp-example/job.trace --row-count 16384 --job-public-inputs tinyzkp-example/job-public-inputs.json --policy tinyzkp-example/policy.json --output-bundle proof.json --state submit-state.json

API keys are shown once. The CLI also accepts an owner-only credentials file and stores resumable non-secret state in mode 0600.

Frozen supported profile

AIR

Goldilocks, Plonky3 0.6.1, degree at most 3, 256 columns, 1,024 constraints, and 8,192 expression nodes.

Trace

Power-of-two row counts from 2¹⁰ through 2²⁴, canonical field values, fixed Zstandard chunks, at most 8 GiB uploaded and 32 GiB expanded.

Admission

At most 2 GiB predicted RSS, 60 minutes predicted wall time, and 70% of currently free worker scratch.

Lifecycle and stable errors

Run quickstart, create a 1,024-row local proof, register the AIR, upload trace chunks, submit, poll, download, and verify. Every mutation uses an idempotency key. Exact retries return the first response; changed-body reuse returns idempotency_conflict.

insufficient_credits

Purchase a top-up or subscription, or submit a smaller workload.

sandbox_fixture_only

Use one of the three signed sample fixtures at no more than 2¹⁶ rows, or purchase PAYG credits.

operationally_contained

New obligations are temporarily disabled. Status, cancellation, verification, balances, Portal access, and completed downloads remain available.

Retention and deletion

Incomplete uploads and input traces are retained for up to 24 hours. Completed PAYG and Builder bundles are retained for seven days, Pro for 30 days, and Scale Beta for 90 days. Account deletion revokes sessions and keys and queues retained artifacts for deletion while immutable billing records remain pseudonymized for reconciliation.
