Terms

Prepaid self-service proving with explicit beta limits.

By using TinyZKP you accept these terms, the published supported profile, the displayed quote and reservation, and the retention policy.

Credits and charging

  • Credits are prepaid; one credit equals one US dollar of hosted proving.
  • TinyZKP reserves 125% of the quote before queueing, settles only an officially verified proof, and releases the remainder.
  • Platform failure, verifier rejection, estimate overflow, and qualifying cancellation release the reservation automatically.
  • There are no automatic overages. Subscription credits expire at the billing-cycle boundary; purchased top-ups remain while the account is active.
  • Refunds reverse unused granted credits. If refunded credits were consumed, new paid submissions may be frozen for operator reconciliation.

Data, retention, and acceptable use

Do not upload regulated, highly sensitive, unlawful, malicious, or irreplaceable data, executable code, credentials, or data you lack authority to process. Input traces may be deleted after 24 hours. Proof bundles are retained for the plan period shown on Pricing. Keep your own copies.

Availability and wind-down

TinyZKP is offered as-is during public beta, may change or be unavailable, and may be wound down. There is no uptime or response-time commitment. If wind-down occurs, TinyZKP will publish status and preserve reasonable access to balances, invoices, Portal cancellation, and retained bundles while addressing unused credits under applicable law and Stripe capabilities.

Support and liability

Support is best effort through the structured operational-request routes. TinyZKP is not independently audited and is not suitable as the sole basis for financial, safety-critical, legal, compliance, or security decisions. Open-source code remains governed by its repository license.
